# Changelog

All notable changes to this project will be documented in this file.

## [v1.1.0] - 2026-08-20

### Added
- Flicker to candles in island buildings, stove market stalls, and braziers.
- Flicker to stoves and smokers.
- Wind affects flicker for lanterns, stoves, and smokers.

## [v1.0.0] - 2026-08-20

### Added
- Flicker to lanterns and island street lights.
- Ability to activate lantern while in inventory (moved from BitsAndBobsRadRedux).
- Lights to the Dragon Cliffs gate (moved from BitsAndBobsRadRedux).
- Ability to disable the ambient player light (moved from BitsAndBobsRadRedux).
